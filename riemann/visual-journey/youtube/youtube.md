The Riemann Hypothesis – A Visual Journey (Primes, Zeta and the Critical Line)

## Description

What do prime numbers have to do with the zeros of a single function? This 3-D visual journey takes you from
prime towers to the zeta function, complex numbers and analytic continuation, all the way to the critical line,
where the Riemann Hypothesis says every non-trivial zero must lie. It is one of the seven Millennium Problems,
worth one million dollars.

Everything you see is computed: the zeta landscape, its zeros and the "music of the primes" built from them.
